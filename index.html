Odoo → Accurate Online integration

GSI Group · IT Staff

  • Python middleware that reads Odoo 19 over XML-RPC (read-only) and posts the full purchase chain (order, goods receipt, vendor bill, down payment) and sales chain (delivery, invoice, down payment) into Accurate the way an admin would enter them by hand.
  • Reconciled daily against the source: 79 of 79 purchase orders correct; payables, receivables and VAT on every synced invoice equal to Odoo; every journal balanced; revenue per account within 1 rupiah.
  • Covers the messy cases: edits after posting, cancellations (closed, never deleted), document numbers reused across two company generations, VAT-inclusive prices with line discounts, next-day posting, retry with back-off on 429/5xx.
  • Mirrored Odoo's 64-account chart of accounts and drove item GL accounts from product categories. A full-scan audit of 4,482 items and 2,594 item-warehouse pairs then showed exactly why cost of goods cannot reconcile without master-data cleanup — evidence presented to management with a risk register.

Accounting data pipelines

GSI Group

  • Two-way integration between Accurate Online's accounting API and Google BigQuery: token plus per-request signature auth, scheduled sync, idempotent loads.
  • Paginated by document id, not by date. A date filter silently skipped records that had been back-dated after creation; sweeping by id closed the gap, and completeness is now verified against the source API's own row count rather than assumed.
  • FIFO cost-of-goods engine reconstructing per-item cost from inventory layers, validated against the accounting journal as referee — 963 of 963 documents matched, where the spreadsheet it replaced matched 49.
  • Read-only XML-RPC bridge into Odoo for downstream reconciliation.

Multi-module ERP

Zuliya Group

  • Dozens of modules in daily production across several business lines: approval workflows, attendance with photo and GPS capture, asset register, cash handling, executive dashboards.
  • Permissions scoped per module and per region, not globally — so one workflow definition serves branches with different rules instead of forking the codebase per branch.
  • Rewrote an executive dashboard's aggregation in SQL after the gateway kept killing it at 86.5 seconds. It runs in 6.0 seconds.
  • MCP server that lets Claude read the ERP directly — document search, which approval desk is backed up, server health (disk, backup age, failed jobs) and gas-meter reading checks — plus ZIA, an in-ERP assistant whose rules the owner edits without a deploy.
  • Security review, then fixes: a second login page that skipped the account lockout and leaked staff names; attendance photos and locations every employee could see; leave and resignation letters readable by 29 accounts, now only HR. Server side: CSP and security headers in nginx, verified Telegram webhooks, working DKIM.
  • Integrations with Telegram (tasks, photo reports, password-reset codes via one-time account linking), a cloud fingerprint-attendance API and map lookups for attendance addresses.
  • Photo-to-data pipeline reading handwritten field forms from phone photos: perspective correction, grid segmentation, per-cell recognition with a voting ensemble — tuned for zero wrong readings rather than maximum coverage, because a silently wrong number costs far more than a blank one.
